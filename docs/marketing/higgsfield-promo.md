**Concept: "Strung by hand. Worn all day."** About 30 seconds, vertical 9:16 for Reels and TikTok.
Act 1 (10 s): the making. Act 2 (15 s): one continuous walking motion, match-cut across six people and six bracelets. Act 3 (5 s): the stack at rest, space for the logo.

**How to get accurate bracelets:** use image-to-video for every shot and upload the matching product photo as the start frame or reference. AI changes bead colours and counts when it works from text alone. Generate each shot separately (5 s each), then cut them together in CapCut. Add the logo and any words in the edit; AI-generated text comes out garbled. Leave out sold-out pieces (The Devotion, The Aurora).
