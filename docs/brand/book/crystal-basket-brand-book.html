01 · The brand

Energy you can wear.

Natural crystal bracelets, chosen by intention, hand-strung in Dubai, cleansed before they leave our hands.

Who it is for

Women in the UAE, mostly 22 to 40, who buy for a feeling, not a trend: calm before exams, confidence before a meeting, something soft for a sister. They shop on Instagram and TikTok, pay with a card or cash on delivery, and gift as often as they treat themselves.

What we promise

Real stone, never dyed or glass. Strung to last, on premium cord, re-strung free if it ever gives. Chosen by intention, so the first question is always "what do you need more of?", never "which stone is trending?".

How it feels

Quiet luxury with warmth. Think of a jeweller's counter in daylight, not a crystal shop at night: white surfaces, ivory linen, one gold bead, one rose accent. Feminine without being pink, spiritual without being mystical.

What we never say

We do not promise that a stone cures, heals or makes anyone rich. Stones are traditionally worn for something. The reminder on the wrist does the work; we say so honestly and it reads as more premium, not less.

03 · Logo rules

Keep it clean

Clear space

Leave empty space around the badge equal to the width of the crystal on every side. Nothing sits inside that space: no text, no other logos, no photo edges.

Minimum size

Full badge: 24 mm in print, 96 px on screen. Below that the name inside is unreadable, so switch to the mark-only version, which holds down to 8 mm or 32 px.

Colour

Ink on white or ivory. Ivory on ink or on a photograph with a dark area. The rose bead stays rose in both. Never gold, never a gradient, never on a busy photo.

Don't

Print it in gold
Stretch or squash it
Put it on a gradient
Rotate it
SALE 50%
Write over it
Recolour the whole mark

04 · Colour

White, ink, one rose bead

The palette is deliberately plain so the stones carry the colour. White is the ground, warm beige is the band that sections the page, ink is for words. Rose is the single accent, used like the one bead in the logo: rarely, and on purpose. Gold is a material, not a colour: it appears as a bead, a hairline or foil, never as text.

Proportions

On any page or post: about 80% white and linen, 15% ink, 4% muted, 1% rose. If rose is noticeable, there is too much of it.

06 · Voice and words

Warm, plain, honest

How we sound

Like a friend who knows stones, not a guru. Short sentences. Second person. We describe what a stone is traditionally worn for and how it feels to wear, then we stop.

Say

worn for · supports · invites · a reminder on the wrist · natural, undyed · hand-strung in Dubai · cleansed before it ships · re-strung free, for life

Never say

cures · heals · guaranteed · manifest money · detox · miracle · 100% effective · medical anything

Naming a bracelet

The [Archetype] — [Intention] Bracelet · [Stones]. The name carries the poetry, the subtitle carries the keywords. The Guardian — Protection Bracelet · Black Tourmaline & Hematite.

Every product, four lines

Tagline

Energy you can wear.

Use it on the hero, the pouch and the bio. Don't add a second one.

The disclaimer

Every product page and the box card carry one line: Crystal meanings reflect traditional beliefs and are not medical advice. Keep it. It protects Alya and it reads as confidence.

08 · Photography

Daylight, skin, linen, one bead of gold

Three shots per bracelet

ShotFrameWhy
On the wrist3:4, natural window light, hand relaxed on linen or marbleAnswers "how big is it?", the number one reason for returns
Product on white1:1, flat circle, soft shadow, centredThe shop tile and the Shopify listing
Macro3:2, beads filling the frame, inclusions visibleProof it is real stone; sells the material

Always

  • One light recipe for every product: soft key from a window, white bounce, no hard flash.
  • Real inclusions, banding and flash left in. Do not retouch the stone to look "perfect"; perfect looks like plastic.
  • Ivory, white and skin tones dominate. Colour comes from the stone.

Never

  • Neon backdrops, smoke, candles, tarot props, purple gel lighting.
  • Stock photos of other people's bracelets.
  • Text on the photo itself. Words live next to the image.
  • Heavy filters. If a filter is needed, the light was wrong.

Props that work

Linen, unglazed ceramic, marble, a single flower, a handwritten card, the pouch. One prop per photo, never a pile.

Until real photos exist

The site runs on AI-generated placeholders made to this brief. Replace them one product at a time; the file names and folders are listed on page 13.

Stories and posts

  • Photos follow page 10. No template frames.
  • Text overlays in Jost, white or ink, small, one line.
  • Highlights: Intentions · Stones · Care · Reviews · Gifting. Cover icons are the mark on linen.
  • Caption formula: promise line, one sentence of stone tradition, the affirmation, then "Link in bio". No hashtag walls; five relevant tags at most.

Discount codes

WELCOME10 for the sign-up popup. Any other code is a word, not a number: CALM, GIFT, EID.
